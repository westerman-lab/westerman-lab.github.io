I'm an Assistant Professor at Massachusetts General Hospital and Harvard Medical School, with a research focus on precision lifestyle and medicine for the prevention of cardiometabolic diseases. I use tools from genomics, epidemiology, and data science to understand why people respond differently to dietary and lifestyle behaviors.

I received my PhD in Biochemical and Molecular Nutrition from Tufts University, and am now a member of the MGH Clinical and Translational Epidemiology Unit, where my work involves the development of methods for large-scale gene-environment interaction analysis and its application in uncovering novel gene-diet and gene-lifestyle interactions. I also hold appointments at the Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard, where I am a member of the Diabetes Initiative and the Diabetes Genetics Research Group, and at the MGH Center for Genomic Medicine.

I co-convene the TOPMed Environmental and Social Determinants of Health working group and am an active member of the CHARGE Gene-Lifestyle Interactions working group.
