We are actively growing, and we are interested in hearing from people who want to work on the genetics of individual variability in response to diet and lifestyle.

## Open positions

### Postdoctoral Fellow

We are recruiting a postdoctoral fellow to work on gene-environment interaction methods and their application to cardiometabolic disease prevention. Depending on interest, projects could center on polygenic score methodology for interaction and response, molecular mechanisms of GxE using metabolomics, or genetic modifiers of response to dietary and weight loss interventions.

A strong candidate will have a PhD (or expect one soon) in genetic epidemiology, statistical genetics, biostatistics, computational biology, nutrition science, or a related field, along with substantial experience in R or Python and comfort working with large-scale genomic data. Experience with biobank-scale datasets, cloud computing environments, or interaction/mediation analysis is a plus but not a requirement.

### Computational Associate

We are recruiting a computational associate to support analysis across the lab's projects. This role suits someone with a bachelor's or master's degree in a quantitative field who wants substantial hands-on experience with human genetics and epidemiology before graduate school or an industry role. Prior experience with R, Python, or shell scripting is expected; genomics experience is helpful but can be learned here.

## Students

We welcome inquiries from graduate and undergraduate students interested in rotations, thesis projects, or research assistantships. Students at Harvard, MGH, and affiliated programs are encouraged to reach out directly. Please note that admissions decisions are made by degree programs rather than by individual labs, so applying to a program is a separate step from contacting us.

## What we look for

* **Curiosity about mechanism.** Our work starts from statistical signals but does not stop there. We care about what is actually happening biologically.
* **Comfort with methodological uncertainty.** Interaction analysis is a domain where the right approach is often genuinely unsettled, and where careful thinking about study design and confounding matters as much as computational skill.
* **Interest in open, reproducible science.** We release software and summary statistics, and we would like everything we do to be usable by others.
* **Collaborative instincts.** Much of our work happens through consortia and cross-institutional collaborations.

Prior expertise in every one of our areas is not expected. A willingness to learn the parts you do not know is.

## How to apply

Email [kewesterman@mgb.org](mailto:kewesterman@mgb.org) with:

* A short note describing your background and what specifically interests you about the lab's work
* Your CV
* For postdoctoral applicants, contact information for two or three references

We read everything that comes in and will get back to you.

## Environment

The lab is based in the [Clinical and Translational Epidemiology Unit](https://www.massgeneral.org/medicine/cteu) at Massachusetts General Hospital, with an additional presence at the [Broad Institute](https://www.broadinstitute.org/) and the [MGH Center for Genomic Medicine](https://www.massgeneral.org/genomic-medicine). Lab members have access to the Boston-area human genetics community, including seminars, working groups, and collaborations across MGH, the Broad, and Harvard.
