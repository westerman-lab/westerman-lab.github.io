We are growing and interested in hearing from people who want to work on projects related to the genetics of individual variability in response to diet and lifestyle. Though dependent on evolving funding, we are interested in candidates for computational associate, graduate student (Master's or PhD capstone/thesis projects), and postdoctoral fellow roles. Lab members have access to the Boston-area human genetics community, including seminars, working groups, and collaborations across MGH, the Broad, and Harvard Medical School.

If interested, email [kewesterman@mgb.org](mailto:kewesterman@mgb.org) with your CV and a short note describing your interest in our work.
